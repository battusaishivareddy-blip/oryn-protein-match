# 🌱 Oryn AI Protein Match

An AI-powered nutrition assistant that helps users discover the best plant-based protein supplement based on their goals, dietary preferences, allergies, budget, and lifestyle.

Built to simplify protein selection with personalized recommendations in under 60 seconds.

---

## 🚀 Live Demo

🔗 https://oryn-protein-match.lovable.app/

## ✨ Features

- 🤖 AI-powered personalized protein recommendations
- 🥗 Supports Vegan and Vegetarian diets
- 💪 Goal-based recommendations (Muscle Gain, Weight Loss, General Fitness, etc.)
- ⚠️ Allergy and ingredient preference filtering
- 💰 Budget-aware product suggestions
- 🌾 Clean ingredient and nutrition-focused recommendations
- 📱 Responsive design for desktop and mobile
- ⚡ Fast and intuitive user experience

## 🎯 Problem

Choosing the right protein supplement can be confusing. Hundreds of products claim to be the best, making it difficult for consumers to find one that fits their health goals, dietary restrictions, ingredient preferences, and budget.

---

## 💡 Solution

Oryn AI Protein Match asks users a few simple questions and delivers personalized recommendations using an AI-driven recommendation engine. The platform helps users make informed nutrition decisions quickly and confidently.

---

## 📋 How It Works

1. Enter your basic information.
2. Select your fitness goal.
3. Choose your dietary preference.
4. Specify allergies and ingredient preferences.
5. Set your budget.
6. Receive personalized protein recommendations.
